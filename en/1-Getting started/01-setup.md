# Get set up in three steps

Install one app, import once, and you're connected. Phones, tablets and computers all work, with no limit on how many.

## Step 1: Install the app

Use the app listed for your device. Download links and each file's fingerprint (SHA-256) are on the console's Download page.

| Device | App | Where to get it |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone, iPad | sing-box MT | App Store. It isn't listed in the China mainland store, so you need an Apple ID from another region. See 「Installing the app on iPhone and iPad」 |
| Android phone or tablet | sing-box, or FlClash | Download page |
| Mac | Clash Verge Rev | Download page. There are separate builds for Apple silicon and Intel; pick the right one |
| Windows | Clash Verge Rev | Download page |
| Linux desktop | Clash Verge Rev (64-bit) | Download page, as a deb or rpm package |

All of these apps are open source, and we only recommend versions we've tested. Download only from the console's links or the official app store: fake copies of popular apps are a common way to steal accounts.

## Step 2: Import

On the device you want to use, open the console's Subscription link page and tap the import button for your app. The app receives all your servers automatically.

- Console on your computer, app on your phone: scan the QR code in the console with the phone app.
- **Don't send your subscription link to yourself through WeChat, QQ or any other chat app.** See 「Your subscription link is your key」 for why.

## Step 3: Connect

Open the app, choose "自动选择" (Auto select), and connect.

Auto select picks the fastest server right now and prefers IPv6. If the network you're on has no IPv6, it switches to the IPv4 backup line within about a minute on its own. For why we built it this way, see 「IPv6 first: why our lines are designed this way」.

## Using several devices

- **Traffic is shared.** All your devices draw from the same plan. You can check usage any time on the console's Usage page.
- **Each device picks its own server.** Your phone and computer can use different servers without affecting each other.
- **Adding a device or switching phones.** Repeat steps 1 and 2 on the new device. You don't need to sign out of the old one first.
- **For your own use only.** There's no device limit, but you can't share the account with others, resell it or post it publicly. If noticeably more devices are online at once than one person normally uses, we'll email you. If they're all yours, you don't need to do anything.
