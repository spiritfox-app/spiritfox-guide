# Your subscription link is your key

Anyone who has your subscription link can use your plan, and we can't tell whether it's you. Guard it like a password.

## Most important: don't send it to yourself through a chat app
Many people send their subscription link to themselves on WeChat or QQ, then open it on another device. That leaves it in the chat history: stored on the chat app's servers, synced to every device you're signed in on, and backed up with your chats. Anyone or anything that can see that history can then take the link. Email, notes and cloud drives "sent to yourself" have the same problem.

**The right way** is for the link to go straight from the console into the app, through nothing else:
- scan the QR code on the console's Subscription link page with the app; or
- sign in to the console on the device you'll use and tap the import button.

## If it leaks
- Someone else uses your traffic and your plan runs out early.
- Their use counts against your account.
- You often only notice when your usage doesn't add up or things slow down.

## When to reset it
- You've ever sent the link via a chat app, email or notes.
- You gave it to someone, or imported it on someone else's device.
- A device was lost, sold or given away.
- The Usage page shows a jump you can't explain.
- Whenever you're unsure. Resetting does no harm.

## How to reset
1. On the console's Subscription link page, click "Reset subscription link", then confirm.
2. The old link stops working right away; devices still using it usually lose their connection within a few minutes.
3. Import again on each of your own devices, the same way as the first time.

Your plan, remaining traffic and expiry date don't change.

## Good habits
- Don't post it in group chats or forums, or share screenshots of it. Friends who want to use the service should get their own account.
- Before selling or giving away a device, delete the app, then reset.
- Glance at the Usage page now and then.
