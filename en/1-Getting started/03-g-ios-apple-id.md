# Installing the app on iPhone and iPad

On Apple devices, use **sing-box MT**, made by the sing-box developers. It isn't listed in the China mainland App Store, so you create an Apple ID for another region and use it only in the App Store to download the app. It takes about 10–15 minutes and doesn't touch your own iCloud, photos or contacts.

## What you need
- **An email address that has never been used for an Apple ID.**
- **Your current +86 phone number.** You don't need a foreign number. Apple says China mainland users can verify with a +86 number.
- **No bank card.** You can choose "None" as the payment method.
- iOS or iPadOS 15.0 or later.

## Step 1: Sign out of the App Store only
1. Open the **App Store**, tap your profile picture at the top right, scroll to the bottom and tap "Sign Out".
2. **Sign out in the App Store only.** Don't sign out from your name at the top of Settings. That's iCloud, and signing out there affects syncing of photos, contacts and notes.

## Step 2: Create a new account for another region
1. On the same screen, tap "Create New Apple Account".
2. **Country or Region:** choose any region outside China mainland.
3. Enter the email and set a strong password you use only here.
4. **Choose "None" for the payment method.** sing-box MT is free; you won't be charged.
5. Some regions ask for an address in the local format. Fill it in as the page asks.
6. Enter your +86 phone number and verify the email and the text message as prompted.

You can also sign up in a computer browser at `account.apple.com`. You'll choose "None" for payment the first time you sign in to the App Store.

## Step 3: Download sing-box MT
1. Search the App Store for **sing-box MT**.
2. Check the ID: `apps.apple.com/app/sing-box-mt/id6785326793`. Apps with similar names are not it.
3. Tap "Get".

## Step 4: Switch back to your own ID and import
1. Once installed, sign out of the overseas ID in the App Store and sign back in with your own. The app keeps working.
2. In your iPhone's browser, sign in to the console and tap the sing-box import button on the Subscription link page.
3. Open the app, choose "自动选择" (Auto select), and connect.

## Common questions
<details><summary>How do I update the app later?</summary>

Apple's rule is that an app updates under the account that downloaded it. To update, switch to the overseas account in the App Store for a moment, update, then switch back.

</details>

<details><summary>There's no "None" payment option?</summary>

Apple requires at least one payment method on accounts that have subscriptions, an unpaid balance, or that organize Family Sharing. That's why you should **create a new** account instead of changing an old one to another region.

</details>

<details><summary>"Your account cannot be created at this time"?</summary>

Try the other way of signing up (App Store or web), or another network, or try again later.

</details>

<details><summary>No verification text message?</summary>

Make sure the country code is +86 and your phone can receive texts, wait a bit, then tap resend. Don't use someone else's number or an SMS-receiving service.

</details>

## Safety
- **Never use an Apple ID someone shared or sold online**, and never sign in to one in Settings. Its owner can lock your phone remotely and see the data you sync.
- Use a strong password on the overseas ID too, and turn on two-factor authentication.

Sources: sing-box documentation `sing-box.sagernet.org/clients/apple/`; Apple Support articles 108647, 111001, 118291.
