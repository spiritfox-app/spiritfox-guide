# Spotting fake sites and fake messages

We never ask for your password, verification code or subscription link by email, text or chat app. Anyone who does is an impostor.

## Common tricks
- **Copycat sites.** One letter changed in the address, or a different ending, with a page that looks identical and asks you to sign in. Everything you type goes to them.
- **Fake "support".** A message or email says your account has a problem and asks for your password or code "to fix it".
- **Fake downloads.** "The same app" offered elsewhere, with account-stealing code inside.
- **Deals too good to be true.** "Lifetime plans" or huge discounts sold outside official channels. We don't sell lifetime plans.

## What we do and don't do
- We don't send you private messages out of the blue.
- We don't ask for passwords, codes or subscription links.
- Support is only in the console's Tickets.
- Codes are bought only through the "Buy a code" button on the console's Plans page, which opens our code store; then come back and redeem under Redeem a code in the console. Don't click "code purchase links" sent from anywhere else.

## Protecting yourself
1. Sign in to the console only at the official address, and bookmark it so you always open it from your bookmarks.
2. Install apps only from the console's Download page or official app stores. The Download page lists each file's fingerprint (SHA-256) so you can check it hasn't been altered.
3. If the browser shows a certificate error or "Your connection is not private", don't continue.

## If you already typed your password into a fake site
1. Change your password right away under Account in the console. If you used the same password elsewhere, change it there too.
2. Reset your subscription link on the Subscription link page.
3. Import it again on your own devices.
