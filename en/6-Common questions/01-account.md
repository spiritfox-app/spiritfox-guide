# Account, plans and traffic

<details><summary>No verification email?</summary>

Check your spam folder and that the address is spelled right. You can resend after 60 seconds; each code is valid for 5 minutes, so use the latest one.

</details>

<details><summary>Can't sign in, account locked?</summary>

After several wrong passwords in a row, sign-in pauses for a while to protect your account. Try again in an hour, or tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in page to set a new one. We can't see or send you your old password.

</details>

<details><summary>Got an "unusual devices online" email?</summary>

We send it when noticeably more devices are online at once than one person normally uses. If they're all yours, you don't need to do anything. If not, change your password under Account first, then reset your subscription link; see 「Your subscription link is your key」.

</details>

<details><summary>Can I try it free first?</summary>

Yes. Every new account gets a trial: 5 GB of traffic for 14 days, excluding US West lines. Try it on your own network at the times you usually use it before deciding to buy.

</details>

<details><summary>How do I buy or renew a plan?</summary>

On the Plans page, tap "Buy a code" to get a code, then enter it under Redeem a code; the plan starts right away. Renewing works the same way: redeem a new code.

</details>

<details><summary>How is traffic counted?</summary>

In real bytes, upload and download both. Most lines have a rate of 1 and US West lines 1.5; see the end of 「Rule mode: what goes direct and what goes through our lines」. All your devices share one plan's traffic, which you can check any time on the Usage page. When you've used 80% of your traffic we'll email you; if you'd rather not get it, turn it off under Email reminders on the Account page.

</details>

<details><summary>What happens when traffic runs out or the plan expires?</summary>

Your connection pauses until you redeem a new code. Your account and settings are kept.

</details>

<details><summary>Can I get a refund?</summary>

No. To better support common payment methods we use a third-party payment provider, and refunds aren't currently supported. Please use the free trial first and buy only once you're sure the service works well on your network. We also suggest paying monthly. If the site runs a special offer and you already trust us fully, then consider paying quarterly or yearly.

</details>

<details><summary>Is there compensation for outages?</summary>

Yes. If the connection service is fully down for more than 4 hours in a row (as recorded on the [status page](https://my.spiritfox.app/en/status.html)), we automatically add traffic to your plan; you don't need to ask. It's calculated per affected day, with part of a day counting as a full day, at one-thirtieth of your plan's monthly traffic per day. For example, a 100 GB plan down for 1 day gets 3.3 GB.

</details>

<details><summary>Can several people share one account?</summary>

No. There's no device limit, but the account is for you alone; it can't be shared, resold or posted publicly. Friends who want to use it should get their own account.

</details>

<details><summary>How do I delete my account?</summary>

Ask in Tickets and we'll delete it within 30 days. All account data is deleted except order records we're required by law to keep for accounting. See the [Privacy Policy](https://my.spiritfox.app/en/privacy-policy.html#your-rights).

</details>

<details><summary>How do I contact you?</summary>

Use Tickets in the console. That lets us deal with your account directly and safely. We never ask for your password by email, text or chat app; see 「Spotting fake sites and fake messages」.

</details>
